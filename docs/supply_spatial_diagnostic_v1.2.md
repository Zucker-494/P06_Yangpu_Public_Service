# Supply Spatial Diagnostic Evidence — v1.2

## Purpose
This stage selects analytically meaningful evidence before final cartography. It deliberately avoids producing a choropleth for every available field.

## Primary map candidates
### Park area per resident
Park count alone treats a very small park and a large park as equivalent. Registered park area per resident therefore carries more information about the magnitude of green-space supply. Park count remains supporting evidence.

### Elderly-care facilities per 10,000 residents
Unlike healthcare centres and community cultural centres, elderly-care facility counts vary substantially across subdistricts. The population-standardised rate is therefore suitable for showing supply variation, while known capacity remains a secondary/partial indicator.

## Supporting chart candidates
### Healthcare centres per 10,000 residents
The current authoritative core layer contains one community health service centre in every subdistrict. The rate therefore mainly expresses population pressure on a fixed one-centre structure rather than variation in facility count.

### Cultural centres per 10,000 residents
The same structural issue applies: one community cultural activity centre is institutionally confirmed for each subdistrict. Mapping the rate is possible, but a chart/table communicates the denominator effect more honestly.

## Sports
The 20-record sports inventory remains district-level evidence. Street assignment is not inferred from incomplete coordinates. It will be incorporated once location validation is adequate.

## Important interpretation rule
Descriptive ordering/ranks in the QA table are not planning priority rankings. No intervention priority is assigned at this stage.

## Cartographic implication
The final map family should answer distinct questions:
1. demand context;
2. magnitude of green-space supply;
3. elderly-care provision;
4. network accessibility;
5. demand–supply gap type;
6. intervention type.

This is preferable to repeating the same choropleth template for every service field.
