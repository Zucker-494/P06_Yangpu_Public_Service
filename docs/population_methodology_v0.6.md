# Population Demand Layer — v0.6

## Frozen baseline
The project uses the Seventh National Population Census (2020) as the currently verified complete authoritative subdistrict-level resident-population baseline.

The 12-subdistrict sum is 1,242,600 residents.

## Temporal control
Official district statistics report 1,199,700 Yangpu residents at the end of 2024.

The district-level change relative to the 2020 subdistrict baseline is -42,900 people (-3.45%).

## Critical rule
The 2024 district total is NOT proportionally redistributed to the 12 subdistricts. Such redistribution would create synthetic precision and could distort demand–supply diagnosis.

## Use in analysis
- `population_total` from the 2020 census may be used for transparent subdistrict-normalised indicators (e.g., facilities per 10,000 residents).
- Results must be described as census-baseline demand estimates, not 2024/2025 street-level population estimates.
- 2024 district population is used only as a temporal-control/limitation indicator unless a newer complete authoritative subdistrict table is verified.
- Migrant-population tables are not substitutes for total resident population.
